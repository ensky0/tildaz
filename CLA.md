## Why this exists

TildaZ is a single-maintainer project. Without a CLA, every contribution
arrives under AGPL-3.0-or-later and only under that license — which means
the project could never be released under any other terms again, because
doing so would require tracking down and getting permission from every
past contributor.

This CLA keeps that door open. It lets the maintainer distribute TildaZ
under different terms in the future — for example, a permissive license, or
a commercial license alongside the AGPL one.

## How to sign

You do not need to sign anything in advance. When you open your first
pull request, an automated check will ask you to confirm agreement by
posting a comment on that pull request:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature is recorded in this repository at
`signatures/version1/cla.json` and covers all of your future
contributions. You only sign once.

If the check does not update after you sign, comment `recheck` on the
pull request.

## Questions

If any term here is a problem for you, open an issue before writing code.
It is better to sort it out first than to have a finished pull request
that cannot be merged.
